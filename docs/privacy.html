Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

DeadLines is a desktop application that turns a course syllabus into an organised plan of assignments and deadlines. It is developed and maintained by Rojus Pauza as an independent, non-commercial project.

The short version. DeadLines has no servers. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer, because there is nowhere to send it. Your courses, your Google profile and your API key are stored only in your own computer's application data folder. The only network requests the app makes are to Google, on your behalf: to sign you in, to read your calendar if you enable it, and to send a syllabus to the Gemini API for analysis using an API key that you supply.

1. Who is responsible

Rojus Pauza. Contact: rojuspauza@gmail.com. DeadLines is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, or by any educational institution.

2. What the app accesses, and why

DeadLines requests the following access from your Google Account. Each is used only for the purpose described.

PermissionWhat it gives access toWhy the app needs it
openid, email, profile Your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile picture URL. To identify which local workspace to open, so that two people sharing a computer keep separate courses, and to show who is signed in.
calendar.readonly Read-only access to your Google Calendar entries: titles, dates, times, locations and the names of your calendars. To display your existing commitments in the same month view as your coursework, so deadlines can be seen in the context of the rest of your schedule. This permission is optional and can be turned off in the app.

DeadLines never requests write access to your calendar. It cannot create, change or delete any event in your Google Account.

3. Where your information is stored

All application data is stored locally on your own device, in the operating system's standard application-data location for DeadLines. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by the developer, because no such server exists.

  • Your Google profile (account identifier, email, name, picture URL) is written to a local file so the app can restore your session and label your workspace.
  • Your Google sign-in token is encrypted using your operating system's secure credential storage — Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows, libsecret on Linux — before being written to disk.
  • Your Gemini API key is encrypted the same way. It is never displayed back to you in full; the app shows only a short masked hint so you can recognise it.
  • Your courses and assignments are stored as plain files on your device, in a separate folder for each Google account that signs in.
  • Google Calendar events are never written to disk. They are fetched when you view a month, held in memory while the app is open, and discarded when it closes.

4. Information sent to third parties

The developer receives nothing. The app communicates only with Google, and only as a direct result of an action you take:

  • Google Sign-In — signing in exchanges credentials directly with Google's authentication servers.
  • Google Calendar API — if you enable the calendar overlay, the app requests your events directly from Google and displays them.
  • Google Gemini API — when you import a syllabus, the text of that document is sent to the Gemini API for analysis, authenticated with the API key you provide. Google's handling of that content is governed by the terms applicable to your own Google account and API key, not by any account of the developer's.

DeadLines contains no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no third-party trackers of any kind. Your information is never sold, rented or shared with anyone.

5. Limited Use disclosure

DeadLines's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the features described above; it is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features; it is not used for advertising; and no humans read it, except where required by law or where you have given explicit consent.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You are always in control:

  • Sign out from within the app clears the local session and asks Google to revoke the token the app holds.
  • Delete local data, in the app's Settings, permanently removes that account's courses, assignments, stored profile and saved API key from your computer.
  • Revoke access entirely at any time from your Google Account permissions page, independently of the app.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its application-data folder removes everything.

7. Security

Credentials are encrypted at rest using your operating system's secure credential storage. All network communication with Google uses HTTPS. Sign-in uses the OAuth 2.0 authorization code flow with PKCE, the flow recommended for installed applications, and your Google password is never seen by or entered into the app — you enter it on Google's own sign-in page in your browser.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and because your data is held on your own device, its safety also depends on the security of that device.

8. Children

DeadLines is intended for students in higher and secondary education and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 — and, as described above, does not collect user information at all.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated date above. Material changes affecting how information is handled will also be noted in the release notes for the version that introduces them.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about how DeadLines handles information: rojuspauza@gmail.com, or open an issue on the project's issue tracker.
