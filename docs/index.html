DDeadLines

Turn any syllabus into a plan.

A free desktop app for students. Drop in a course syllabus and get an editable plan with every deadline in it.

Download for macOS

What it does

Reads your syllabus

Point it at a PDF or Word syllabus. Google's Gemini reads the prose, the tables and the course calendar, and builds a course with every graded item it can find.

One calendar for everything

Every deadline across every course in a single month view, colour-coded per course, with items that need checking flagged.

Shows its working

The imported items appear beside the original document, so you can check what it found against the source before anything is added.

Stays on your machine

There is no DeadLines server. Your courses and your API key live in your computer's application data folder, and nothing is sent to us — because there is no "us" to send it to.

Getting started

  1. Download and open the app. It is not yet notarised by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm the first launch. The disk image explains how.
  2. Sign in with Google. Used to identify your workspace, and — if you allow it — to show your existing calendar events alongside your coursework.
  3. Add a Gemini API key. Free to create at Google AI Studio. Reading syllabi is billed to your own Google account, not to anyone else's.
  4. Import a syllabus and review what it found.
